! CraftBrowsing default network filter rules.

! Scope: network filters only. Element hiding and other cosmetic filters are not
! applied by the browser yet, so nothing cosmetic belongs in this file.
!
! These are curated endpoints, not a redistribution of a third-party list. A
! subscription updater (EasyList / EasyPrivacy / uBlock filters, with cached
! downloads the user triggers) is a separate roadmap item; until then this file
! is the whole ruleset and `craft-adblock` reports how many rules it compiled.
!
! Login and share widgets are deliberately left alone: connect.facebook.net,
! accounts.google.com, recaptcha and Turnstile endpoints are functional, so they
